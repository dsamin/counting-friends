Privacy Policy — Counting Friends

Last updated: June 16, 2026

Counting Friends collects no data. It stores nothing about you or your child on any server, and it transmits nothing off your device — ever.

  • No ads
  • No in-app purchases
  • No data collected
  • No way to fail

What we collect

Nothing. Counting Friends has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no servers. We do not collect your name, your child's name, an email address, a device identifier, location, usage data, or anything else. We could not look up your child if we wanted to, because there is nothing to look up.

What stays on your device

The only things the app remembers are your own preferences, and they never leave your device. Saved locally in your browser or app:

  • the difficulty you last chose (Easy, Medium, or Hard);
  • an optional child's name, if you typed one;
  • your reduce-motion setting;
  • whether the voice is on or off.

These are stored on your own device only and are never sent anywhere. Clearing your browser data, or deleting the app, removes them completely.

No third parties

There are no analytics tools, no advertising networks, no third-party SDKs, and no trackers of any kind. No outside company receives any information about you or your child, because no information ever leaves the device.

Children's privacy

Counting Friends is designed for children. Because it collects, stores, and transmits no data, it is compliant by design with children's privacy laws including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU's GDPR rules for children (GDPR-K). The optional child's-name field stays on the device and is used for one thing only: to personalize the spoken praise (for example, "Three! You got it, Jayden!"). It is never transmitted, shared, or stored anywhere but your device.

No accounts, no network

There is nothing to sign up for and no way to log in. Once the app has loaded, it works fully offline and makes no network requests. There is no back end for it to talk to.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and post the new version here. Because the app collects no data, we do not expect material changes — but if one happens, it will appear here.
